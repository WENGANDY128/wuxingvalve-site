Structural Features

  1. The sealing surface is hardfaced with Stellite alloy.
  2. The disc is provided with a small hole to balance the medium pressure in the valve cavity, reducing the operating torque.
  3. An “O”-type sealing element is installed between the disc and the stuffing box liner, providing good sealing performance.
  4. Manufacturing and acceptance shall comply with JB450.
