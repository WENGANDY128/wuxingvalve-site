Structural Features

  1. The connection ends adopt a butt-welded design.[cite: 5]
  2. Integral body structure, compact appearance, and no leakage.[cite: 5]
  3. The disc and stem are integrated, and the medium passage inside the body is smooth, reducing pressure loss.[cite: 5]
  4. The stem is of rising type, improving the sealing performance of the packing.[cite: 5]
  5. Valves larger than DN50 are equipped with an impact handwheel.[cite: 5]
  6. Manufacturing and acceptance requirements comply with JB450-1992.[cite: 5]
