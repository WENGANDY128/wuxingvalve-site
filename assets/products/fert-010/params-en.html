Structural Features

  1. The sealing surfaces of the disc and seat are hardfaced with Stellite alloy.
  2. An “O” ring is installed between the bonnet, seat and body to prevent medium leakage.
  3. The disc slides up and down in the guide hole of the bonnet under medium flow and spring force.
  4. Manufacturing and acceptance requirements conform to JB450.
