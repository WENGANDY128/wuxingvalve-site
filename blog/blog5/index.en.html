Empowering Global Industry: Wuxing Valve Highlights Technical Strength at the Wenzhou Valve Exhibition

2026-10-18 Source: International Trade & Marketing Dept. Tags: Wenzhou Valve Exhibition / Industrial Valves / Pneumatic Actuators / High Pressure Forged Steel

The China (Wenzhou) International Pump & Valve Exhibition serves as one of the most critical epicenters for industrial fluid equipment and manufacturing innovation globally. As a trusted manufacturer and exporter with decades of commitment to high-precision engineering, Wuxing Valve presented its complete suite of industrial valve solutions at this grand event, attracting extensive interest from international buyers and engineering contractors.

1. Next-Gen Automation: Pneumatic Tank Bottom Discharge Valves

Designed for immediate discharge in reactor vessels and storage equipment, our featured automatic pneumatic bottom discharge valves demonstrated superior speed and reliable sealing capabilities:

  • Heavy-Duty Pneumatic Actuators: Driven by high-thrust red pneumatic cylinders, ensuring thousands of rapid cycle tests without delay or mechanical binding.
  • Anti-Clogging Design: Crafted from investment-cast stainless steel with a downward-opening disc, preventing sediment accumulation at vessel outlets.

2. Precision Under Extreme Conditions: Forged High-Pressure Needle & Globe Valves

For chemical plant applications and ultra-high-pressure pipelines, Wuxing Valve showcased its array of forged steel instrument components:

  • Integrally Forged Integrity: Free from internal casting porosity, offering exceptional structural resilience under pulsating high-pressure fluid environments.
  • Vibration-Resistant Blue Nut Locking: Engineered with high-tensile fasteners and blue anti-loosening washers to ensure continuous leakage protection under severe pipe vibration.

3. Complete Pipeline Integration: High-Precision Forged Flanges & DN200 PN40 Globe Valves

To deliver a complete fluid management ecosystem, Wuxing Valve demonstrated its standardized manufacturing capability:

  • ASME/DIN Compliant Flanges: Precision-machined flange faces and bolt hole spacing ensuring seamless on-site bolt alignment.
  • DN200 PN40 Cast Steel Globe Valves: Coated with high-durability sky-blue anti-corrosion paint and fitted with high-torque handwheels to reduce manual effort.
