Craftsmanship & Precision, Hardcore Delivery: Direct from the Export Site of Large Batch Industrial Valves

2026-06-29 Source: Manufacturing Department / QC Center Tags: Factory Capability / Pneumatic Discharge Valve / Forged High-Pressure / International Standards

As a senior supplier deeply rooted in the fields of industrial valve manufacturing and international trade, Wuxing Valve has always adhered to rigorous international standards to guide our production. Today, the workshop welcomed the centralized inspection and delivery of this month's core export orders. Leveraging our comprehensive modern machining capabilities, rigorous testing methods, and flexible customization capabilities, we have once again delivered a perfect response to our overseas clients.

1. Automated Control: Automatic Pneumatic Bottom Discharge Valves (Fig. 1)

Targeting bottom fluid control and high-concentration media discharge conditions for reactors and storage tanks, we delivered pneumatic discharge valves equipped with bright red efficient pneumatic actuators:

  • Precision Drive: Equipped with high-precision, large-thrust pneumatic cylinders, which passed tens of thousands of opening and closing fatigue tests before leaving the factory to ensure zero jamming and zero delay under harsh working conditions.
  • Structural Advantages: The valve body is precision cast from stainless steel and features an downward-opening structure, completely solving the issue of media accumulation at the bottom of the vessel. Meanwhile, each valve is equipped with an air filter regulator and limit switches to high standards.

2. Ultra-High Pressure Control: Precision Forged Steel Needle / Globe Valve Array (Fig. 2, Fig. 4)

For rigorous high-pressure instrument systems and power plant grade pipelines, Wuxing Valve demonstrated its robust mass manufacturing capabilities for forged steel products:

  • Massive Batch Manufacturing (Fig. 2): The high-pressure forged steel needle valves, lined up in rows, are integrally forged to eliminate porosity defects found in traditional casting processes. The valve stem threads are roll-strengthened, and the valve seat sealing surface is overlaid with hard alloy, specifically designed for high-pressure, high-frequency shut-off.
  • Premium Materials & Anti-Loosening Details (Fig. 4): For high-pressure globe valves manufactured from special stainless steel materials, all middle flange connection bolts are coated with signature high-strength blue anti-loosening washers and nuts. Even in pipeline networks with high vibration or severe temperature differences, they can maintain continuous tightness to achieve zero external leakage sealing standards.

3. Complete Matching Support: Large Batch High-Precision Forged Flange Components (Fig. 3)

As a comprehensive foreign trade supply chain, we provide not only high-quality valves but also custom high-standard flange connectors for our clients. The high-precision machined forged flanges, neatly stacked in the finished product area, strictly implement ASME / DIN / GB standards. The sealing surface machining texture is delicate, and the bolt holes are precisely aligned, ensuring a 100% first-time success rate for on-site pipeline connection.

4. Heavy Pipeline Weapons: Large-Diameter Cast Steel Globe Valve Series (Fig. 5)

The DN200 PN40 large-diameter cast steel globe valves neatly arranged on-site are ready for shipment:

  • Standard & Protection: The valve body casting is dense, perfectly bearing high-pressure impacts. The exterior uses high-grade industrial sky blue anti-corrosion baking paint, possessing excellent resistance against salt spray and outdoor weathering.
  • User-Friendly Operation: Equipped with a large impact-type handwheel featuring clear "OPEN / SHUT" direction indicators, greatly reducing the manual opening and closing torque under large diameters and high pressure differentials.
